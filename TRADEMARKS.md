## What you may do without asking

- Refer to the software as "AntennaFox" to identify it, including in articles, talks, package descriptions and
  compatibility statements ("works with AntennaFox", "imports AntennaFox sessions").
- Redistribute unmodified official releases with the name and logo intact, including as a package in a
  distribution or package manager.
- Link to the project, quote it, and use screenshots of it.
- Fork the code and distribute modified versions under the GPL, as long as the result is not presented as the
  official AntennaFox or as published or endorsed by Altinum Technologies e.U.

## What requires a different name

If you distribute a modified version, give it its own name and do not use the fox logo as its identity, so that
users are not misled about who produced the software they are running. A clear statement such as "based on
AntennaFox by Altinum Technologies e.U." or "unofficial build of AntennaFox" is welcome; using the AntennaFox name or
logo as the product name or icon of the modified version is not. Builds that you make from unmodified official
sources for your own organisation may keep the name.

## What you may not do

- Use "AntennaFox", the fox logo or "Altinum Technologies" as the name or logo of a different product, service,
  company or domain name, or in a way that suggests that Altinum Technologies e.U. produced, endorsed or supports your
  product.
- Alter the logo (other than scaling it) and present the result as the official logo.
- Use "Altinum Technologies®" in a way that implies affiliation with the publisher.
